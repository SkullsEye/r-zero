Built for the Razorpay AI Buildathon, Track 02. Data: IEEE-CIS Fraud Detection (Vesta Corporation), replicated on the ULB European card dataset. Method after Ogata's epidemic-type aftershock model, Wald's sequential probability ratio test, Hooi et al.'s camouflage-resistant weighting and Deb et al.'s NSGA-II.

Cost parameters are illustrative defaults — ₹1,200 per chargeback, ₹40 per review, ₹970 for wrongly stopping a customer. Every rupee figure moves with them. Nothing you put into this page is uploaded anywhere.
