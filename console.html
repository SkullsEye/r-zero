Contagion intensity across the held-out period

Average decayed proximity to confirmed fraud, against fraud actually observed.

The measured reproduction number is R₀ = 0.438: each fraud triggers roughly another 0.44 within minutes, then the burst dies out. Below 1 an outbreak fades on its own; above 1 it grows without limit. That half-life is the reason proximity to fraud has to be measured at a moment rather than as a fixed property of the graph.
